Shop Information

Clear, simple details about buying from Koresque.

Delivery

All Koresque e-books are digital products. There is no physical shipping.

Digital downloads via Payhip

Our store and checkout are provided by Payhip. Prices are in Australian dollars. Once your payment is complete, Payhip sends an email with your download link instantly. Books are supplied as PDF files that open on most phones, tablets and computers. Keep your email so you can download your book again when you need it.

Refunds

Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law apply to every purchase. If a file is faulty, will not download, or is significantly different from its description, contact us and we will arrange a replacement file or a refund. We are also happy to help with any other concern about your order, so please get in touch.

Privacy

This website itself does not collect personal information or use forms. When you buy or download through Payhip, Payhip processes your name, email and payment details to complete your order, under Payhip's privacy policy. We use your details only to provide your purchase and support, and we do not sell your information.
