Your worth already lives within you.

Inner Poise

A gentle way of sitting or standing. It expresses posture, presence and quiet confidence.

  1. Sit with your feet flat, or stand with your feet hip-width apart.
  2. Let your spine lengthen gently.
  3. Let your shoulders soften.
  4. Let your belly be soft and relaxed.
  5. Let your breath move freely.

The Returning Breath

Breathe in and out through your nose, easy and unforced. Let each breath return you to yourself.

If you like, count gently as you breathe in and as you breathe out. Let one breath flow into the next.

Go gently and move at your own pace. Stop if anything feels uncomfortable. If you are pregnant, check with your health provider first.
