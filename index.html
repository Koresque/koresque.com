About Koresque

A Moment for You

Each Koresque book offers gentle moments to explore in your own time.

Inner Poise

Sit or stand in a way that is comfortable. Let your spine be long, your shoulders soft, and your belly soft and relaxed. Notice how your body feels.

The Returning Breath

Notice your breath as it is. Let it come and go easily, in its own time, and let your attention come back to yourself.

Move within what feels comfortable for you, and pause whenever you like.
