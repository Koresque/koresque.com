About Koresque

A sense of being begins within.

Koresque supports women in reconnecting with themselves through education, reflection and everyday practices that honour the whole woman.

Many parts of a woman's wellbeing are often considered one at a time. Koresque brings them together, recognising the connection between body, mind and spirit.

Our guiding philosophy is simple: Breathe, Balance, Become. Breathe to return to the present. Balance to honour your time. Become to express who you truly are.

“I created Koresque from a belief that a woman’s wellbeing begins at her core. Too often, women’s health is divided into separate concerns, when our bodies, emotions and experiences are deeply connected.”

“Your worth already lives within you.”

— Daphne Morgan

Koresque invites you to recognise that worth and nurture it, gently and in your own way. The journey begins with our first e-book, and there is more to come.
