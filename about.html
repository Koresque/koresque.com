About Koresque

A sense of being begins within.

Koresque supports women in reconnecting with themselves through education, reflection and everyday practices that honour the whole woman.

Many parts of a woman's wellbeing are often considered one at a time. Koresque brings them together, recognising the connection between body, mind and spirit.

Our guiding philosophy is simple: Breathe, Balance, Become. Breathe to return to the present. Balance to honour your time. Become to express who you truly are.

“I created Koresque from a belief that a woman’s wellbeing begins at her core. Too often, women’s health is divided into separate concerns, when our bodies, emotions and experiences are deeply connected.”

“Your worth already lives within you.”

— Daphne Morgan

Koresque invites you to recognise that worth and nurture it, gently and in your own way. It begins with The Origin: Embrace Your Core Essence, and there is more to come.

The Koresque Creator

Daphne Morgan

I created Koresque from a belief that a woman's wellbeing begins at her core. Too often, women's health is divided into separate concerns, when our bodies, emotions and experiences are deeply connected.

As a woman, mother of six and grandmother of five, I feel privileged by the life I've lived and the women I've shared it with. Through my writing, I hope to create space for women to understand themselves, feel supported and care for themselves as a whole.

My children are four sons and two daughters, and my grandchildren are four boys and one girl. To raise my children and watch my family grow has shown me how differently each person experiences life, and how much each of us needs a centre of our own.

My beautiful husband and I have shared a long marriage. It has taken work and sacrifice, and it still does. Understanding what matters to each of us, and how we support one another, has taught me a great deal. I believe that understanding our roles and purpose is fundamental to harmony, in a marriage, in a family and in a life.

For about ten years I was unwell and did not know why. I was given several separate diagnoses, and many of my symptoms were put down to menopause. Each label gave me something to hold on to, yet none of them felt like the full answer. Through those years I held my breath, breathed shallowly and lost touch with my own sense of worth.

In the end the cause was found: fibroids that had grown over a decade or more. The decision to have a hysterectomy took me three years. I deeply respect medicine and the people who practise it. I needed to feel heard and informed enough to make my own choice, and that took patience, questions and time. I made the decision from my centre, and I stand by it.

My search for answers showed me a gap in holistic care for women, and in the information offered before and after an operation. Women receive excellent care in pieces. What I wanted, and what I could not find, was a way to see the pieces together.

All of it led me back to my centre. My core was my everything, and Koresque was born from that. These books are how I share what I found, so that other women can find their centre sooner and use it with purpose.

Koresque has a simple promise in three words: Breathe, Balance, Become. Breathe is the first of them. It is where I began, and I believe it is where every woman can begin.

I share this as my lived experience, in the hope that these pages offer you good company and a clear direction.

Three pathways

Breathe

Breathe reconnects you with your breath and your core, the centre of your Core Essence, and teaches one simple practice for engaging the power of your core.

Balance

Balance takes the power of your core into daily life, across roles and purpose, Time Craft, authenticity, rest and shared load, with clear takeaways in every chapter.

Become

Explore your values, recognise your uniqueness and express your authentic self.
