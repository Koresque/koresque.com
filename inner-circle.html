The Koresque Kitchen Table

Pull up a chair, the kettle's on

So much gets talked over at the kitchen table, with a cup of tea or coffee and a biscuit. The Inner Circle offers members a seat at that table. Here you are heard without judgement or bias, and you receive company and perspective shared from lived experience. Never advice, and never diagnosis.

What's on the table for members

Letters to Koresque

Write in privately and anonymously with whatever is on your mind, the way you might over a second cup of tea.

The monthly members' newsletter

Each month the newsletter answers themes from the letters for everyone. No names are ever included.

Around the Table Coming soon

A moderated members' space where Koresque replies and members can respond under a first name or a chosen name.

Members-only downloads

Printable pages and extras, set out just for members, like a plate of biscuits for the table.

Early access to new volumes

First pour of each new volume of the Koresque Kollection.
